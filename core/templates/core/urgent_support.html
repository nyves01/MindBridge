Urgent and emergency support

If someone is in immediate danger

Contact local emergency services or go to the nearest hospital or health facility. Do not wait for a MindBridge appointment or response.

Rwanda-specific crisis contacts

Verified, current Rwanda-specific crisis telephone numbers and organisations are awaiting confirmation from reliable official or institutional sources. No unverified contact details are shown here.

While you seek help

  • Move away from immediate danger if it is safe to do so.
  • Stay with a trusted person and ask them to accompany you.
  • Use a local, qualified healthcare provider or emergency service.
  • Share the situation clearly and ask for immediate support.

MindBridge Rwanda is educational and does not provide emergency, diagnostic, or crisis-response services.
